About Me

Hi, I'm Dan! Thanks for checking out my site! I am a cognitive scientist, behavioral researcher, and data analyst.

Across scientific and consumer/market research roles, I have over 10 years of experience in both quantitative research (experimental design, statistical modeling, data visualization, survey research, automatic text analysis) and qualitative research (focus groups, ethnographies, in-home and in-store testing, etc.).

I love turning data into insights and finding ways to communicate complex findings in clear and engaging ways!

I received my PhD in cognitve psychology and an MS in statistics from Northwestern University in June 2023. My scientific research interests include analogical reasoning, language processing, and metaphor comprehension.

I'm now seeking to use my research and data analysis training in a career in data analytics / data science.
